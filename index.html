About the club

The Heath's Vryburg M.C.C. is a young, energetic motorcycle club based in the heart of Vryburg, North West. We've been riding strong for just over a year, and we're still small, still friendly and still growing, one ride at a time.

We love social rides, joy rides and good times on two wheels. Whether you're a seasoned rider or new to the road, you'll always find open arms and positive vibes with us.

What we stand for

Friendship first

Ready to ride with the crew?

Here's how joining the club works.

  1. Meet the clubMessage us on WhatsApp and come say hi.
  2. Ride with usJoin us on a few rides and get to know the crew.
  3. Get voted inYou're presented at a club meeting, and the members vote you in.
  4. Prospect periodAs a prospect, you take part in a set number of rides and rallies.
  5. Full patchThe club votes again, and you earn your full patch.
Message us on WhatsApp

Who can join

Any rider with a valid licence and a roadworthy bike, from any experience level.

Questions

I'm a new rider. Is that okay?

Absolutely. We ride at an easy pace and look out for each other.

Do I need a big bike?

Bring what you ride. All bikes are welcome.

Do I have to come to every ride?

Not every ride. As a prospect you'll need to make a set number of rides and rallies. Otherwise, come when you can.

What does it cost?

Come on a ride first, with no obligation. Message us and we'll explain membership when you're ready.
